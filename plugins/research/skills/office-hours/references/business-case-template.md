## 0. State of the Project
  - Has there any research being made so far? How deep is the market expertise?
  - Who is involved in the project and who does what?

## 1. The one-liner

Who has what problem, and what are you proposing to do about it. One or two
sentences, no solution-jargon.

## 2. Target user (specific, not a persona-poster)

- Who exactly (role, context, company size / life situation)
- Why them and not an adjacent group
- How to find/reach 10 more of them

## 3. The problem, in their words

- What triggers the problem (the "push" moment — JTBD switch trigger)
- What they currently do about it — see Section 4
- What it costs them if unsolved (time, money, risk, status)

## 4. How they solve it today

- Workarounds (spreadsheets, Fiverr, manual process, "just deal with it")
- Why those are insufficient — the actual gap, not a wished-for gap
- Switching cost: what would it take for them to drop the current approach
  
## 5. Brief market overview

- Direct vs. indirect competitors
- Rought market size
- What changed recently in the market or why didn't this get solved five years ago?

## 6. Where users hang around

- Communities/channels with evidence
- Where do users talk about the problem, where to outreach users?

## 7. Validation signal log (append-only, dated)

The single most important section. Every real-world touchpoint gets one
line, tagged by strength. Strong = money, time, or reputation on the line.
Weak = a like, a "cool idea," a survey response. Never delete rows — append
only, so the trend over time stays visible.
