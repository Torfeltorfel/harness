## Domain expertise depth
- Walk me through every existing solution people in this market actually use today — named products, not categories. For each one: what exactly does it get wrong? Not "too expensive" or "clunky" — the specific workflow, feature, or moment where it fails the user.
- Tell me about a real person who tried one of these and dropped it. What broke, in their words?
- If these solutions are basically fine, why hasn't someone already fixed this with what's on the market?
- Can they name 3 real prospects or contacts in this market?

**Red flag:** vision talk instead of critique — "the space is ripe for disruption," "nobody's really solved this," "we can build something 10x better." That's a pitch, not expertise. Push until you get a teardown of a specific product's specific failure.
